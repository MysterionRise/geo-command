# Consigliere Assessment 1

## Findings

1. **CRITICAL — Beta lifecycle ambiguity.** The draft does not bind one manifest per UTC day, replay/re-entry behavior, scheduled-round reuse, reserve substitution, the Day 7 cutoff, or the Day 8 access block.
2. **MEDIUM — Gate-phase ambiguity.** Pre-recruitment gate preparation, post-Day-7 reporting, report recommendation, and continuation decision are conflated.
3. **HIGH — Durable truth gap.** Broad citations to Grilling rounds are not backed by sufficiently detailed durable conclusions.
4. **HIGH — Access and operator security incomplete.** Credential expiry, replay resistance, protected storage/logging, reveal authorization, least privilege, privileged-action audit, and authoritative-endpoint abuse controls are not objectively bound.
5. **HIGH — Privacy lifecycle incomplete.** Retention maximums, deletion deadlines, propagation, derived-data treatment, backup expiry, and restore behavior are insufficiently bounded.
6. **HIGH — Rights/evidence eligibility incomplete.** Minimum source-specific evidence, independent approval, attribution obligations, takedown, correction, and legal-deletion evidence are not objectively bound.
7. **HIGH — Answer-release boundary incomplete.** The draft does not define answer states, per-player reveal entitlement, or rejection of premature/cross-participant reveal requests.
8. **HIGH — Acceptance coverage incomplete.** Multiple high-risk functional, non-functional, architectural, and out-of-scope clauses lack explicit evidence and approvers.
9. **MEDIUM — Subjective terms remain.** “Plausible,” “short-lived,” “bounded,” “spoiler-free,” and reviewer independence need frozen definitions, measurable bounds, or approval artifacts.
10. **LOW — Product frame and no-code rule pass.** The GeoGuessr-for-code framing is coherent and the draft contains no implementation code or pseudocode.

## Required Revisions

1. Bind the complete 14-day UTC lifecycle, manifest uniqueness, re-entry, substitution, freeze/report timing, and Week 2 access block.
2. Split pre-recruitment readiness, post-Day-7 report, and Don continuation acceptance.
3. Persist detailed accepted Grilling decisions and use exact durable citations.
4. Add outcome-based credential, reveal, operator, and authoritative-endpoint security requirements.
5. Require a launch-blocking data schedule for purpose, access, retention, withdrawal/deletion, propagation, backups, and restore.
6. Bind source-specific rights/evidence records and independent approval evidence.
7. Define answer states and reveal authorization with negative-path acceptance tests.
8. Add a requirement-to-acceptance matrix with objective evidence and named approvers.
9. Replace subjective terms with frozen policies or explicit prelaunch approval artifacts.
