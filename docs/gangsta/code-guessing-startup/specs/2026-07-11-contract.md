# Contract: Code Mystery Arcade Beta

## Objective

Build a closed-beta “GeoGuessr for code” browser game. A player is dropped into an unfamiliar code location and infers where they are in code space from progressively revealed evidence. Provenance, programming language, algorithm, and famous-project mysteries are coordinate systems over that single location-and-inference promise, not unrelated quizzes. The Don explicitly selects the controlled-product-experiment direction: this programme delivers the shared arcade and a controlled provenance/language beta; algorithm and project are staged, and country attribution is excluded. This programme charter governs six separately signable child Heists so that delivery remains executable without weakening the shared product promise. — Source: SRC-001 §§ Objective, Product Hypothesis, Mode Triage; SRC-002 §§ Resume Context, Key Decisions; DEC-001; DEC-002.

## Normative Definitions

- **Beta day:** a numbered half-open UTC interval beginning at 00:00:00 and ending immediately before 00:00:00 the next day.
- **Manifest lineage:** the complete immutable version history for one beta day.
- **Current issuance version:** the sole manifest version within a lineage eligible to be newly bound at a given instant.
- **ISSUED:** a manifest becomes ISSUED when its immutable version identifier is atomically bound to a participant-day session in authoritative storage. Replacement eligibility is determined by ISSUED status.
- **DISPLAYED:** a round becomes DISPLAYED when the authoritative service accepts its first-display event from the bound session. DISPLAYED supports operational evidence but never decides replacement eligibility.
- **Participant lineage:** the pseudonymous continuity record joining an approved invitation, consent, enrollment, daily sessions, withdrawal, and credential status without storing recruitment identity in gameplay telemetry.
- **Correction notice acknowledgement:** an authoritative participant action confirming that a VOID or CONTENT_WITHDRAWN notice was presented; it does not imply agreement or waive rights.

## Requirements

### Functional Requirements

1. **FR-001 — Core experience.** Each round shall present unfamiliar code, permit a hidden-identity inference, offer progressive evidence, accept one answer, and return an evidence-backed reveal. — Source: Dossier §§ Objective, Product Hypothesis; Final Grilling Checkpoint § Product Frame and Scope.
2. **FR-002 — Fourteen-day lifecycle.** The beta shall contain exactly fourteen active UTC release days, identified Day 1 through Day 14. Day 1 begins at 00:00:00 UTC on a Don-approved start date. Every beta day is the half-open interval from 00:00:00 through, but not including, 00:00:00 the next day. A pause between Day 7 and Day 8 does not consume an active release day; Day 8 begins at the first 00:00:00 UTC after a Don-signed OPEN decision. — Source: SRC-002 §§ Beta and Content, Timing, Access, and Availability; SRC-003 Finding 1; SRC-008 B-010.
3. **FR-003 — Daily manifest lineage.** Each active beta day shall have exactly one manifest lineage. A lineage may contain multiple immutable versions created only by a recorded correction or reserve promotion. At every instant, exactly one version shall be eligible for new issuance. Each version shall contain five distinct round positions: exactly three provenance and two language. Every participant-day session shall remain permanently bound to the immutable version ISSUED to it, subject only to the VOID and CONTENT_WITHDRAWN correction rules. A scheduled round shall appear in no more than one beta-day lineage; a reserve becomes scheduled only through recorded promotion. — Source: SRC-002 §§ Product Frame and Scope, Beta and Content; DEC-003; SRC-008 B-003.
4. **FR-004 — Re-entry, grace, and replay.** A participant may resume an incomplete daily session against the same ISSUED manifest version and orthogonal state until sixty minutes after that UTC day ends. Credentials for that day shall not be issued after the day ends and shall expire at the earlier of twenty-six hours after issue or the end of the sixty-minute grace period. Answers and authoritative events accepted within grace are attributed to the prior day. After grace, an incomplete session status becomes EXPIRED and read-only; new answers/events are rejected, and the started-but-incomplete lineage remains in the frozen denominator. After completion, re-entry is read-only and creates no new answers, scores, completion events, or gate observations. — Source: SRC-004 Finding 1; SRC-008 B-004 and B-005.
5. **FR-005 — Progressive clues.** Each round shall expose a base excerpt and no more than two ordered clues. Each accepted clue shall apply the frozen beta scoring penalty before the answer is accepted. — Source: Dossier § Recommended MVP Scope; Final Grilling Checkpoint § Product Frame and Scope.
6. **FR-006 — Provenance semantics.** A provenance answer shall identify the recorded source class: recorded model output and, only when DEC-005’s rights gate is affirmative, recorded Stack Overflow publication; otherwise recorded model output and project-owned human sample. It shall never claim original authorship, human versus AI identity, code quality, or scientific detection. The active candidate labels and source regime shall be versioned, frozen before invitations, and identical across all provenance rounds on all fourteen active days. — Source: SRC-001 §§ Product Hypothesis, Risks and Unknowns 4–5; SRC-002 §§ Product Frame and Scope, Scoring and Measurement; DEC-005.
7. **FR-007 — Language semantics.** A language answer shall use a versioned closed candidate set with canonical labels, aliases, answer, candidate count, distractor rationale, and calibration record. Two independent technical reviewers shall agree that one answer is defensible; any competing defensible answer makes the round ineligible. — Source: Dossier §§ Mode Triage, Risks and Unknowns 7; Final Grilling Checkpoint §§ Product Frame and Scope, Beta and Content; Consigliere Review 1 Finding 9.
8. **FR-008 — Orthogonal authoritative state.** State shall be stored in five independent dimensions: session status is NOT_STARTED, IN_PROGRESS, COMPLETED, EXPIRED, or WITHDRAWN; round interaction status is UNSEEN, OPEN, ANSWERED, or REVEALED; correction status is ACTIVE, VOID, or CONTENT_WITHDRAWN; clue state is zero, one, or two clues revealed; and answer state is either no accepted answer or one accepted answer with immutable answer identifier and acceptance timestamp. An answer may be accepted only when session status is IN_PROGRESS, interaction status is OPEN, correction status is ACTIVE, and credential, UTC, and grace rules pass. REVEALED requires an accepted answer. VOID and CONTENT_WITHDRAWN reject new answers. Every permitted and prohibited cross-dimension transition shall be frozen and verified. — Source: SRC-003 Findings 4 and 7; SRC-004 Finding 1; DEC-004; SRC-008 B-005.
9. **FR-009 — Reveal authorization.** A reveal request shall succeed only for the same participant lineage, beta day, manifest version, round, and accepted-answer transition. Premature, expired, replayed, cross-lineage, cross-day, cross-manifest-version, or cross-round reveal requests shall be rejected and audited without disclosing answer data. — Source: Consigliere Review 1 Findings 4 and 7.
10. **FR-010 — Evidence-backed reveal.** An authorized reveal shall include correctness, required attribution, source evidence approved for display, explanation of helpful/misleading signals, and content, candidate-set, scoring, and rules versions. — Source: Dossier §§ Product Hypothesis, Recommended MVP Scope; Final Grilling Checkpoint § Beta and Content.
11. **FR-011 — Entertainment results.** The product shall provide an entertainment-only session result and spoiler-free share output. Streak eligibility shall be calculated solely from authoritative UTC beta-day completion. The UI may display corresponding dates and times in the participant’s locale, but localization shall not alter streak calculation. Spoiler-free output shall contain no answer, code, clue, source, language, content identifier, attribution, or reveal URL. — Source: SRC-001 § Recommended MVP Scope; SRC-002 §§ Scoring and Measurement, Timing, Access, and Availability; SRC-003 Finding 9; SRC-008 B-011.
12. **FR-012 — Content corpus.** Before Day 1, a readiness report shall prove eligibility of seventy scheduled rounds and fifteen reserves, preserving the three-to-two ratio. Ineligible, quarantined, or legally excluded rounds shall not count. — Source: Final Grilling Checkpoint § Beta and Content.
13. **FR-013 — Reserve substitution and quarantine branches.** Before a manifest version is first ISSUED, an eligible reserve of the same mode and approved difficulty band may replace a round through a new immutable version in the same lineage. If an affected version was ISSUED but nobody answered the round, its bound sessions transition the round’s correction status to VOID or, when deletion requires, CONTENT_WITHDRAWN; those participants acknowledge the notice and finish with an adjusted maximum, protected streak, and analytical exclusion. Participants without an ISSUED version for that day receive the current issuance version containing a recorded reserve promotion. If anyone answered, no participant receives a silent replacement: the round becomes VOID or CONTENT_WITHDRAWN for all versions in the lineage. Answered participants retain immutable historical score facts but see the correction; unanswered participants acknowledge the notice. Current results exclude the affected round, reduce the maximum, and show a spoiler-free void symbol. The round/day total is excluded from correctness, clue, and score analysis; completion remains valid after all non-void reveals and notice acknowledgement. CONTENT_WITHDRAWN exposes only a generic notice. DISPLAYED status is recorded but never controls replacement eligibility. — Source: SRC-005 Finding 1; SRC-008 B-003 and B-004.
14. **FR-014 — Minimum common evidence record.** Every content item shall record stable ID, source class, content hash, excerpt, acquisition method/date, evidence reference, creator or source identity where lawful, ownership/license/authorization basis, reviewer identities/dates, eligibility decision, attribution or disclosure text, correction state, and publication status. A project-owned human sample shall additionally identify its creation/commission basis and recorded project authorization. — Source: Dossier §§ Content and Rights Constraints, Recommended MVP Scope; Consigliere Review 1 Finding 6; DEC-005.
15. **FR-015 — Stack Overflow evidence record.** Every Stack Overflow item shall additionally record source URL, post ID, revision ID, author, contribution/revision date, applicable license and version, acquisition basis, first-display-attribution decision, and approved reveal attribution. — Source: Dossier § Content and Rights Constraints; Final Grilling Checkpoint § Beta and Content; Consigliere Review 1 Finding 6.
16. **FR-016 — Model-generation evidence record.** Every model item shall additionally record provider, model, generation date, prompt provenance or approved redacted evidence, available generation parameters, raw-output hash, provider-terms version, generating account or plan, commercial-use basis, applicable data-use or training setting, known provider restrictions, similarity/contamination review result, reviewer third-party-rights decision, approved public attribution or disclosure text, and acquisition/reviewer decision. — Source: SRC-001 § Recommended MVP Scope; SRC-002 § Beta and Content; SRC-003 Finding 6; SRC-008 B-007.
17. **FR-017 — Independent eligibility approval.** Publication requires recorded approval of answer integrity, ambiguity, difficulty, provenance, rights, attribution, secrets, personal data, safety, inert rendering, accessibility, and evidence minimization. For each item, the Content Editor, Technical Reviewer A, Technical Reviewer B, and Rights/Safety Reviewer shall be four different named people; the two technical reviewers shall be qualified for the item’s mode and may review multiple modes when their qualifications are recorded. — Source: SRC-002 §§ Beta and Content, Safety, Privacy, and Operations; SRC-003 Findings 6 and 9; SRC-008 B-012.
18. **FR-018 — Stack Overflow pre-content rights gate and fallback.** No Stack Overflow content may become eligible until a Don-approved written rights determination states whether attribution at reveal satisfies the applicable license for the exact presentation and interaction design. It shall identify the reviewer, scope, contribution/revision-date treatment, license versions considered, attribution format, share-alike treatment, and effective date. Items requiring attribution on first display remain excluded. If no affirmative determination exists, the provenance beta shall use only project-owned human samples and project-authorized model outputs, and Stack Overflow content shall remain excluded. 19. **FR-019 — Takedown and correction intake.** A monitored complaint route shall acknowledge every report within forty-eight elapsed hours. A credible critical report shall trigger provisional quarantine within four elapsed hours and a credible high report within twenty-four elapsed hours. “Critical” means credible ongoing exposure of unlawful content, secrets, personal data, or imminent security/safety harm; “high” means credible material rights, provenance, privacy, or safety harm without the critical immediacy; medium/low definitions and examples shall be frozen in the rights policy. Critical/high content remains unavailable pending decision. A documented independent decision/escalation occurs within seven calendar days; medium/low within fourteen. The deciding reviewer shall be different from the original Content Editor and original Rights/Safety approver. Each case records severity, evidence, quarantine, correction, deletion, escalation, completion, and missed-deadline rationale. — Source: Consigliere Review 3 Finding 3.

#### Scoring and candidate handling

- Every mode uses a base value of 1,000 points per round. Accepting clue one permanently subtracts 200 points; accepting clue two permanently subtracts a further 300 points. A correct answer therefore earns 1,000, 800, or 500 points according to clue use; an incorrect answer earns zero. A round result cannot fall below zero.
- These point values are frozen by Revision 6 and remain unchanged for both modes and all fourteen active days. Corrections alter the attainable session maximum through FR-013; they do not rewrite immutable historical answer facts.
- Every answer record carries the candidate-set version and candidate count presented for that round. Chance baseline is calculated as one divided by that presented count. Mode-specific analysis uses candidate count directly and never treats combined entertainment points as comparable ability.
- Candidate labels, aliases, ordering policy, correct candidate, and distractor rationale are immutable within a candidate-set version. Ordering shown to a participant shall be deterministic for the bound session or recorded when randomized.

#### Denominators and formula inputs

| Measure | Frozen denominator and required inputs |
|---|---|
| Mixed-session completion | Included participant-day sessions with a SESSION_STARTED event; numerator is those reaching COMPLETED after all ACTIVE rounds are revealed and all required correction notices are acknowledged |
| Voluntary return | Included participant-day opportunities where the participant started the preceding active day, remained consented/eligible/unrevoked through a full next active beta-day observation window, and was not blocked by a platform incident; numerator is a SESSION_STARTED event on that next active beta day |
| Transition continuation | Included sessions that reached a terminal interaction for round position one through four; numerator is first display of the immediately following active round position |
| Mode abandonment | Included ACTIVE rounds first DISPLAYED in that mode; numerator is rounds without an accepted answer before session expiry or participant withdrawal, with VOID/CONTENT_WITHDRAWN rounds excluded |
| Mode clue use | Included ACTIVE rounds first DISPLAYED in that mode; numerators are rounds accepting at least one clue and rounds accepting both clues |
| Response sufficiency | Included participants offered the versioned survey after the defined trigger; numerator is accepted eligible submissions before freeze |
| Shared-promise comprehension | Accepted eligible submissions to the frozen closed-response comprehension items; numerator is responses classified as understanding the shared code-location promise by the pre-signed scoring key |
| Critical-defect status | All critical defects opened on or before freeze; reported by resolved/unresolved state and release-blocking decision, never as a rate when no defect exists |

Every formula consumes only immutable event-schema, eligibility, consent, incident, correction, candidate-set, mode, day, position, cohort, instrument, and formula versions. A participant begins as PENDING for analysis; becomes INCLUDED only after valid consent, eligibility, non-tester classification, and SESSION_STARTED; and becomes EXCLUDED prospectively and in frozen analysis upon authenticated withdrawal, invalid eligibility, operational-tester classification, duplicate lineage, or signed integrity exclusion. Exclusion never deletes or rewrites immutable operational audit facts; it records reason, effective time, approver, and formula treatment. Round-level VOID/CONTENT_WITHDRAWN exclusion does not automatically exclude unaffected session completion. All formula outputs shall expose raw numerator, denominator, missing count, excluded count by reason, and version identifiers.

Provider/framework choices remain Resource Development decisions. — Source: Dossier § Architectural Direction for Debate; Final Grilling Checkpoint § Timing, Access, and Availability.

3. **NFR-003 — Answer containment.** Correct-answer data before an authorized REVEALED transition, future answers, and restricted evidence shall not enter public bundles, source maps, prefetch data, analytics, or logs. — Source: SRC-002 §§ Timing, Access, and Availability, Safety, Privacy, and Operations; SRC-003 Finding 7; DEC-004.
4. **NFR-004 — Credential security.** Daily-session credentials shall be scoped to one lineage, UTC day, manifest version, and permitted transitions; shall expire at the earlier of twenty-six hours after issue or sixty minutes after that UTC day ends; shall use protected transport/storage; shall be absent from URLs/logs/analytics; and shall resist replay. They become unusable after credential revocation, participant withdrawal, or expiry. Round VOID/CONTENT_WITHDRAWN state narrows the credential to the correction notice and unaffected transitions; it does not itself revoke the credential. — Source: Consigliere Review 4 Finding 1.
5. **NFR-005 — Endpoint authorization.** Every authoritative endpoint, including enrollment, manifests, state, clues, answers, reveals, events, withdrawal, deletion, correction, and operator actions, shall enforce input bounds, authorization, allowed transitions, anti-forgery, rate limits, expiry, and audit outcomes appropriate to its role. — Source: Final Grilling Checkpoint § Timing, Access, and Availability; Consigliere Review 1 Finding 4.
6. **NFR-006 — Operator security.** Privileged actions shall require authenticated named operators, least privilege, role-appropriate authorization, and append-only audit records for content approval, publication, quarantine, correction, deletion, restore, gate freeze, report approval, and continuation decisions. — Source: Final Grilling Checkpoint § Safety, Privacy, and Operations; Consigliere Review 1 Finding 4.
7. **NFR-007 — Inert rendering.** Code shall render as escaped inert text. Explanations shall use sanitized non-scriptable formatting. External links shall use approved HTTPS URLs, referrer suppression, and opener isolation. Deceptive bidi/zero-width controls shall be rejected unless visibly annotated and independently approved. — Source: Final Grilling Checkpoint § Safety, Privacy, and Operations.
8. **NFR-008 — Accessibility.** The full five-round flow shall support keyboard-only use, stable visible focus, announced dynamic changes, screen-reader labels, readable contrast, non-color-only state, text-equivalent code/answer state, responsive layout, and reduced motion. — Source: Dossier § Recommended MVP Scope; Final Grilling Checkpoint § Safety, Privacy, and Operations.
9. **NFR-009 — Data minimization.** Telemetry shall exclude raw code, prompts, free text, IP-derived fingerprints, full user-agent strings, secrets, and recruitment identity. — Source: Final Grilling Checkpoint §§ Scoring and Measurement, Safety, Privacy, and Operations.
10. **NFR-010 — Retention ceilings and clocks.** For each copy, the earliest calculated deadline controls. Raw gameplay events and recruitment bridges are deleted by thirty days after the earlier of beta close or authenticated withdrawal. Provider/CDN/application logs are deleted by thirty days after log creation, or earlier when a propagated request/provider rule requires. Temporary exports/support copies are deleted by seven days after creation or authenticated request, whichever is earlier. A project-controlled backup expires by thirty-five days after creation and data subject to a propagated deletion shall not survive later than thirty-five days after propagation. Restricted evidence is deleted by ninety days after the earliest of beta close, item withdrawal, or an applicable authenticated request. A legal hold names approver, purpose, fields, start, thirty-day review cadence, and expiry no later than 180 days unless qualified counsel renews it in writing. — Source: Consigliere Review 3 Finding 2.
11. **NFR-011 — Evidence security.** Restricted evidence shall be minimized, redacted, encrypted under project control, role-limited, access-logged, excluded from public repositories/bundles, and subject to the data schedule and deletion propagation. — Source: Final Grilling Checkpoint § Safety, Privacy, and Operations; Consigliere Review 1 Finding 5.
12. **NFR-012 — Provider inventory.** Before deployment, every store, log, backup, export, support system, operator copy, and subprocessor shall have a Don-approved purpose, processing/consent basis where applicable, field list, access owner, retention ceiling, deletion capability, propagation rule, backup expiry, and restoration behavior. — Source: Final Grilling Checkpoint § Safety, Privacy, and Operations; Consigliere Review 1 Finding 5.

14. **NFR-014 — Delivery freshness and availability.** New manifests and reveals shall fail closed when authoritative revocation freshness exceeds five minutes. The active-beta service availability target shall be at least 99% measured per minute; platform-caused invalid periods shall not break entertainment streaks and shall be reported in the gate. — Source: Final Grilling Checkpoint § Timing, Access, and Availability; Consigliere Review 1 Finding 9.

18. **NFR-018 — Browser, viewport, and assistive-technology support.** At the pre-recruitment freeze, the support matrix shall name the then-current versions and cover the latest two stable major versions of desktop Chrome, Edge, and Firefox; the latest two stable major versions of Safari on macOS; the latest two stable major versions of Safari on iOS/iPadOS; and the latest two stable major versions of Chrome on Android. The complete flow shall work at viewports from 320 by 568 CSS pixels upward without loss of required content or controls. JavaScript is required for gameplay; without it, the service shall present an accessible static explanation and no partial or misleading game state. Required assistive-technology combinations are VoiceOver with Safari on current supported macOS and iOS, and NVDA with Chrome and Firefox on current supported Windows. — Source: SRC-008 B-014.
19. **NFR-019 — User-facing performance and mutation safety.** The beta-load profile is 100 concurrently active participant sessions and a sustained twenty authoritative requests per second for fifteen minutes. Under that profile, the cached static shell shall have an origin/CDN-boundary p95 response time below 500 milliseconds; manifest load, clue acceptance, answer acceptance, and reveal authorization shall each have p95 server response time below 750 milliseconds. A beta excerpt is limited to the first boundary reached: 400 rendered lines or 32 KiB of UTF-8 text. On every NFR-018 browser family, using a reference client with four logical processor cores, 4 GiB available memory, 10 Mbps downstream bandwidth, 100-millisecond round-trip latency, and the 320 by 568 CSS-pixel viewport, the maximum excerpt and required controls shall become visually complete and keyboard-usable within 1,000 milliseconds p95 after payload receipt; input-to-next-paint latency for scrolling, clue activation, answer selection, and reveal navigation shall remain below 100 milliseconds p95. Server measurements require at least 1,000 completed observations per endpoint; client measurements require at least 100 completed observations per browser family. No authoritative mutation shall be automatically retried without an idempotency key, and repeated use of the same key shall create at most one accepted transition. A measured target miss is FAIL and blocks invitations; an insufficient sample is INDETERMINATE and blocks invitations. — Source: SRC-008 B-015; SRC-010 Finding 3.

1. **AD-001 — GeoGuessr-for-code frame.** Code is a location; progressive evidence helps the player infer where they are. Modes are coordinate systems over that shared experience. — Source: DEC-001; SRC-002 §§ Resume Context, Product Frame and Scope.
2. **AD-002 — Two playable beta modes.** Provenance and language are active; algorithm/project are later amendments. — Source: Final Grilling Checkpoint § Product Frame and Scope.
3. **AD-003 — Mixed sessions.** Every manifest guarantees three provenance and two language rounds. — Source: SRC-002 §§ Key Decisions Passed to The Sit-Down, Product Frame and Scope.

5. **AD-005 — Curated versioned content.** Content is manually acquired through approved channels, independently reviewed, versioned, and distributed in revocable packs. — Source: Dossier § Content and Rights Constraints; Final Grilling Checkpoint § Beta and Content.

## Reviewer Compatibility and Minimum Staffing

For any one content item, all four content roles below are mutually incompatible. The same qualified person may review different items or multiple modes, but may fill only one of the four roles for a given item.

| Role on an item | May also be Editor | May also be Technical A | May also be Technical B | May also be Rights/Safety | Qualification requirement |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Content Editor | Not applicable | No | No | No | Content preparation and evidence-record training |
| Technical Reviewer A | No | Not applicable | No | No | Recorded competence for the item’s mode |
| Technical Reviewer B | No | No | Not applicable | No | Recorded competence for the item’s mode |
| Rights/Safety Reviewer | No | No | No | Not applicable | Don-approved rights/safety qualification or counsel status where required |

The minimum viable beta staff is six named people: enough to fill the four independent item roles plus a primary and backup Release Operator. Security Reviewer, Accessibility Reviewer, Data Steward, Product Owner, complaint decider, and operational backup duties may be assigned among those six only when the assignee’s competency is recorded and the following separations remain true: a complaint decider is not the affected item’s original Editor or Rights/Safety approver; an operator does not approve their own privileged-action audit; and restore/reconciliation is verified by a person other than the person performing the restore. If six qualified people and these separations are not available, Day 1 is blocked.

## Grilling Conclusions

### Key Decisions

- GeoGuessr for code is the durable product thesis.
- The Don explicitly selected the controlled-product-experiment direction: beta one deliberately tests a mixed provenance/language arcade, while famous-project guessing remains staged.
- Revision 6 is a programme charter delivered through six separately gated child Heists.
- Every session has three provenance and two language rounds.
