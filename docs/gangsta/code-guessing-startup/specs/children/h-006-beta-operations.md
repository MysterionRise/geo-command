# Child Contract H-006: Beta Operations and Measurement

## Objective

Operate and evaluate the fourteen-active-day controlled beta, including release manifests, bounded telemetry, frozen formulas, performance evidence, incident/outage treatment, the Day 7 continuation gate, and the pre-recruitment package.

## Inherited Programme Clauses

Primary clauses: FR-002 through FR-004, FR-011 through FR-013, FR-019, FR-020, FR-027 through FR-035, the complete Measurement Interface Contract, NFR-006, NFR-009, NFR-012 through NFR-019, AD-003, AD-006 through AD-010, all parent acceptance criteria, all out-of-scope constraints, and all accepted risks.

## Child Requirements

1. Assign immutable release identifiers to event schemas, formulas, and scoring/instrument artifacts that implement the already-frozen parent semantics without alteration; freeze and sign the permitted cohort, eligibility/observation windows, thresholds, final survey wording, manifests, exclusions, outage treatment, freeze time, incident rules, and report template before invitations.
2. Operate fourteen midnight-bounded active UTC days with a Day 7 grace/freeze, explicit report, hard Day 8 block, and signed OPEN decision.
3. Emit only the frozen authoritative event families and fields, apply inclusion/exclusion transitions without rewriting audit facts, and reproduce every numerator, denominator, missing count, exclusion, and version.
4. Keep entertainment scoring separate from provenance/language chance-aware analysis and report every coherence measure without causal or public-retention claims.
5. Meet availability, freshness, browser, accessibility, performance, sample, idempotency, incident, correction, complaint, and backup operational requirements.
6. Prove all negative capabilities and keep implementation/recruitment blocked whenever a parent or child gate is incomplete.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Every parent AC-001 through AC-019 has fresh attributable evidence from the responsible child territory.
- Day 7 PASS, FAIL, and INDETERMINATE formulas and OPEN/DO NOT OPEN/PAUSE recommendations reproduce exactly; measured misses and insufficient evidence remain distinct.
- The pre-recruitment gate, runbook, incident drills, source/version inventory, and six-child dependency ledger are signed and internally consistent.
- No invitation is issued and no child implementation is released before its applicable signatures and prerequisites.

## Dependencies

H-006 integration requires accepted interfaces and evidence from H-001 through H-005. Planning and schema review may begin earlier, but the pre-recruitment gate cannot complete until every upstream child passes.

## Out of Scope

Public launch, acquisition/monetization validation, advanced scaling, public social features, future modes, and any claim beyond the controlled cohort.

## Implementation Authorization

The child signatures are complete. Work-package release remains blocked until Resource Development closes and the Don separately authorizes The Hit.
