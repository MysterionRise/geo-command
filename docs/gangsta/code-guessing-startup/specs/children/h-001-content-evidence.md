# Child Contract H-001: Content and Evidence Pipeline

## Objective

Create the project-controlled content operations needed to produce, review, version, approve, quarantine, correct, and withdraw the seventy scheduled and fifteen reserve rounds without exposing restricted evidence. This child owns content eligibility, not gameplay delivery.

## Inherited Programme Clauses

Primary clauses: FR-012 through FR-019, the content-operation portion of FR-032, NFR-007, NFR-011, NFR-015 through NFR-017, AD-005, AD-008, DEC-005, AC-004 through AC-006, AC-011, AC-015 through AC-017, and risks R-002 through R-005 and R-009 through R-010. All applicable security, privacy, rights, source-truth, and negative-scope clauses remain inherited even when not repeated.

## Child Requirements

1. Define immutable common, Stack Overflow, model-output, and project-owned-human evidence records that contain every parent field and reject incomplete eligibility decisions.
2. Enforce the four-person per-item independence matrix and record qualifications, conflicts, decisions, dates, and evidence versions.
3. Keep Stack Overflow ineligible unless the exact FR-018 written determination is approved; otherwise operate the project-owned-human/model fallback without changing the parent source regime after invitations.
4. Produce a readiness ledger proving seventy scheduled and fifteen reserve rounds with the required three-to-two mode ratio, uniqueness, difficulty bands, and reserve compatibility.
5. Provide complaint, provisional-quarantine, correction, CONTENT_WITHDRAWN, cache-purge, deletion, and immutable-history operations within the parent deadlines.
6. Keep restricted evidence encrypted, access-limited, logged, absent from public artifacts, and covered by retention and restore rules.

## Acceptance Criteria

- Parent AC-004, AC-005, AC-006, AC-011, AC-015, AC-016, and applicable AC-017 checks pass with fresh evidence.
- Every eligible item resolves to immutable content, evidence, reviewer, candidate, clue, correction, and renderer-compatible versions.
- The Stack Overflow determination path and fallback path are each exercised; exactly one is active for the beta source regime.
- Critical and high complaint drills prove forty-eight-hour acknowledgement, four-/twenty-four-hour provisional quarantine, and seven-day decision/escalation. Separate medium and low drills prove forty-eight-hour acknowledgement and fourteen-day decision/escalation, plus reviewer separation, notices, purge, and deletion behavior.

## Dependencies

Entry dependency: approved programme Contract Revision 6. H-002 consumes H-001’s immutable content/reveal interfaces; H-003 and H-004 cannot complete before those interfaces are signed.

## Out of Scope

Browser gameplay, participant identity, beta analytics, live scraping, arbitrary execution, public branding, and future algorithm/project content.

## Implementation Authorization

The child signatures are complete. Work-package release remains blocked until Resource Development completes, WP-001 prerequisites pass, and the Don authorizes The Hit.
