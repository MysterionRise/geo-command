## Accepted Detailed Grilling Conclusions

### Product Frame and Scope

- The single durable promise is “GeoGuessr for code”: the player is dropped into unfamiliar code, infers a hidden identity from evidence, and learns which signals helped or misled.
- Beta one is a controlled two-mode expression of the broader arcade. Provenance and language are playable; algorithm and famous-project modes are staged; country attribution is excluded.
- Every session contains three provenance rounds and two language rounds. Modes share the session shell but retain separate answer, clue, evidence, explanation, difficulty, and analytical semantics.
- Provenance describes recorded source only. It never establishes original authorship, human versus AI identity, code quality, or scientific detection.

### Beta and Content

- The beta is planned for 14 UTC release days with a Day 7 continuation decision.
- Seventy scheduled rounds and fifteen ratio-preserving reserves must be fully eligible before Day 1.
- The gate limits participant exposure, operational cost, reputational harm, and legal/safety risk; it does not save the initial editorial investment.
- A round is eligible only after structural, technical, ambiguity, difficulty, provenance, rights, secret, personal-data, safety, rendering, accessibility, and independent approval checks pass.
- Any Stack Overflow excerpt requiring attribution on first display is excluded from provenance gameplay.
- Content evidence is minimized, redacted, access-controlled, retention-bound, and separated from public delivery.

### Scoring and Measurement

- Combined scoring is entertainment only. Mode performance is analyzed separately against its candidate count, chance baseline, clue policy, day, position, and cohort.
- The gate package is frozen before recruitment and includes event definitions, formulas, sample/response floors, thresholds, survey instruments, content manifests, exclusion rules, outage treatment, data cutoff, incident rules, and report format.
- Enrollment, consent, eligibility, start, activation, round completion, session completion, return, survey response, withdrawal, and analytical inclusion are distinct states or measures.
- Hypothetical preference between mixed and single-mode play is exploratory unless participants receive real comparison experiences.
- The exact fixed-cohort membership rule is deliberately deferred but must block recruitment until Don-signed.
- The Don owns the Day 7 continuation decision. Whether FAIL or INDETERMINATE may be overridden remains deliberately deferred; no silent override is permitted.

### Timing, Access, and Availability

- UTC is the sole release, return, and streak boundary; device time, location, travel, and daylight-saving changes do not alter analytical eligibility.
- Accountless invitations map to pseudonymous participant lineages; recruitment identity is separately controlled and minimized.
- Consent, eligibility, recruitment identity, lineage, credentials, withdrawal, and analytical inclusion are separate states.
- Credential reissue requires control of the original recruitment channel or documented verification, preserves approved lineage continuity, and revokes predecessor and descendant credentials.
- The authoritative service controls manifests, reveal entitlement, consent state, revocation, and bounded event intake. New manifests and reveals fail closed when authoritative freshness cannot be verified.
- Revocation prevents future delivery and purges controllable caches but cannot recall content already rendered, copied, cached, or screenshotted outside project control.

### Safety, Privacy, and Operations

- Code renders only as inert escaped text. Explanations and links are sanitized. Unsafe schemes, scriptable markup, and deceptive control characters are prohibited or explicitly surfaced and independently approved.
- Every application store, log, backup, export, support system, operator copy, and subprocessor requires a purpose, owner, retention, deletion, and restoration rule.
- Restored data cannot become reachable until consent-withdrawal, deletion, and revocation ledgers are replayed and reconciled.
- Content Editor, Rights/Safety Reviewer, Release Operator, Data Steward, and Don are operational hats. Rights/safety approval is independent from item preparation; emergency release/quarantine and restore operations require named backup coverage.
- Beta success is directional evidence of controlled arcade coherence and early return only. It does not validate acquisition, monetization, market size, public retention, or causal claims.
